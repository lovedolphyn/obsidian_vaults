A chamber in the heart of **Enigram Mountain** that contained a wondrous device. They brought it back with them to the community. Dubbed the **Taker of Sorrow**, this ancient mechanism grants peace to a troubled, guilty, or otherwise anguished mind. How it does so isn’t quite understood, but that hardly matters, given that those who emerge from the machine do so with a smile, as if seeing the world for the first time.
