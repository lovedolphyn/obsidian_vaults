# Ending the Adventure
>- If the PCs **work with [[Weymel]] instead of killing him**, they probably do so grudgingly, but they can begin to **use [[songbirds]]** to lure and capture teratomas that have spread across the landscape. [[Weymel]] **admits everything** and even seems relieved that it is finally over. 

>- If the PCs **kill [[Weymel]]**, they can also **destroy the [[Taker of Sorrow]]** and **end the threat** of new [[teratoma]] creation. Doing so immediately **releases** all those who had come under **[[Weymel]]’s sway**, and the victims are grateful, though confused and unsure of how to proceed.

>- Alternatively, the PCs can **kill [[Weymel]]** but **attempt to modify the [[Taker of Sorrow]]** on their own. PCs who are ==versed in understanding and/or crafting numenera== have a *good shot* at success.

- Regardless of how they **end the threat**, if the PCs bring back a **good story** to the **[[Aeon Priest Talosian]] in [[Vesburgh]]**, he is as good as his word. 
>[!success] Loot:
>He grants the PCs **as many cyphers as they can safely carry**, **plus an [[artifact]]**, not to mention about **150 shins** stuffed in a chest. 

>- If **[[Divine Nex]] survives** the adventure, the aerosaltant is **extremely happy** with the PCs and thanks them warmly. The performer eventually **creates a rhyme or song** that features the characters as **heroes**. This performance becomes part of [[Divine Nex]]’s **regular lineup** and is eventually **learned and performed** by **others** in the Ninth World.

>[!success] Loot:
>**Experience Point Awards**: 
>
>If the PCs successfully 
>- **protect Divine Nex** over the course of this adventure and **stay true to their shin obligation**, they each <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">gain 1 XP</mark>. 
>- If they **protect [[Vesburgh]]**, they <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">gain 2 XP</mark>. 
>- If they **destroy the Taker of Sorrow**, they <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">gain 2 XP</mark>, but **if they fix it** and try to **convert** as many [[teratoma]] packs **back into people** as possible, they <mark style="background: #ADCCFFA6;">earn 4 XP</mark>. 
>
>These awards are in addition to any XP earned from GM intrusions and discoveries.
