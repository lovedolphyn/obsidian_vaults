#### Teratoma Packs (Babble Mites): 
![[Creature - Teratoma filled corpse.jpg|right hsmall]]
**[[Teratoma Packs (Babble Mites)|Teratomas]]** are also called babble mites. ==A [[teratoma]] pack, composed of five to ten horrific creatures==, is all that remains of a single person whose mind didn’t accept the mental conditioning [[Weymel]] introduced using the [[Taker of Sorrow]]. 

In a literal sense, their minds (and bodies) were torn apart by that conflict, though their flesh lives on. With their half-formed mouths or gaping larynxes, the creatures hum, speak nonsense, rhyme, whisper urgently, and sing with scratchy, distorted voices. These sounds are not made consciously. 

Mostly, [[teratoma]] packs want to hunt in darkness and bring down lone prey on which to feast in bloody secret. Though each [[teratoma]] begins its “life” as a relatively small chunk of flesh, the more one feeds, the more one grows.
