#### **The Serein** and the [[Taker of Sorrow]]: 
[[Gram]] also hosts a community of ascetics who call themselves the serein. Decades ago, those who would become founders of the serein discovered a chamber in the heart of Enigram Mountain that contained a wondrous device. They brought it back with them to the community. ==Dubbed the [[Taker of Sorrow]]==, this ancient mechanism grants peace to a troubled, guilty, or otherwise anguished mind. How it does so isn’t quite understood, but that hardly matters, given that those who emerge from the machine do so with a smile, as if seeing the world for the first time.

All was well until about ten years ago, when an unscrupulous serein named ==[[Weymel]]== decided to use the [[Taker of Sorrow]] to better his standing in the community. He tinkered with the machine, with only limited understanding of how it worked in the first place. He tried to modify the minds of supplicants who used the Taker so that, in addition to feeling good about other things, they would also feel good about him. Perhaps they would even look upon him with adoration and a desire to do as he suggested.

In this, [[Weymel]] was only partially successful. Supplicants left the machine with the same lightness of being as before, and with him suddenly transfigured in their minds. But a few emerged with headaches and no love for [[Weymel]]. And within just a few hours, these supplicants dissolved into lumpy, horrific animated growths—==called **babble mites** by some, [[Teratoma Packs (Babble Mites)|teratoma]]== by others—that scampered off into the night.
