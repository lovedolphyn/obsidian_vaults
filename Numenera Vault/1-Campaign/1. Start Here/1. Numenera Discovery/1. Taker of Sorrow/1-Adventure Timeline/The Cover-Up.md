#### The Cover-up: 
[[Teratoma]] packs and the rash of [[Gram]] citizen disappearances troubled the town for almost a full year. But the babble mites were finally expelled into the surrounding lands, and [[Weymel]] obtained the means to cast out any new packs that formed. 

As more and more citizens eventually fell under [[Weymel]]’s sway due to the [[Taker of Sorrow]]’s influence, he was able to channel their uncertainty about disappearances into a story that reinforced his own power. He created, from whole cloth, the tale of a spiritual entity in [[Gram]] that moves against those who secretly hide sins against another. Now, if a mother, a child, a healer, or someone else suddenly goes missing with no explanation, it’s because the **Reaper of Sin** came for them. The Reaper, [[Weymel]] teaches, is an instrument of the community’s purity and continued tranquility.
