*The characters discover the aftermath of a savage tussle between a [[Ravage Bear]] and a [[teratoma]] pack.*

The characters are moving along the top of a ravine that forms an almost perfect V; the nadir of the ravine lies about 50 feet (15 m) lower than the lip. Here and there, the drit and vegetation have crumbled away to reveal an underlying crystalline surface, in which tiny lights like stars seem to dart and streak.

Along the way, they find a cavity from which a great stink wafts, smelling of rotting meat. Besides the terrible smell, the sounds of someone crying—and maybe a few other sounds like voices—emerge from the cave mouth. Characters can investigate or simply go on their way. 

##### Teratoma-Infested Corpse: 
![[Creature - Teratoma filled corpse.jpg|right hsmall]] Investigation reveals a cavity about 20 feet (6 m) across that contains the corpse of a large tusked beast (a [[Ravage Bear]]). A pack of horrifically animated lumps of tumorous flesh feed with makeshift mouths and teeth.

Though not immediately visible, **someone** in the chamber is whispering <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“So, so good. So tasty!”</mark> and variations thereof. **Another** voice squalls, ==sounding almost like a child crying inconsolably==. And **a third** mutters, on and on, <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“I was happy, once. Joyous! Without sorrow, once,”</mark> and so on. 

With a bit of observation, PCs determine that the human-like voices are coming from the animate tumorous blobs as they eat the corpse. **[[Creature - Teratoma filled corpse.jpg|Show to players]]** These creatures are called [[Teratoma|teratomas]].

```encounter
name: Teratoma Surprise
creatures:
  - 5: [[Teratoma]]
```

##### Teratoma Surprise: 
>[!faq] Unless the PCs ==attempt and succeed at stealthily withdrawing==, the [[teratoma]] pack boils out of the corpse **en masse** and goes for the closest character. Attempts to communicate with the teratomas fail utterly. At this point, unless the PCs have had some previous interaction with the creatures, they likely can’t discover too much more about them unless a PC brings some sort of divination ability or [[cypher]].

>[!danger] **GM group intrusion**: 
>The [[Ravage Bear]]’s mate returns from a hunt to find the PCs in its lair, and it attacks from the rear.
