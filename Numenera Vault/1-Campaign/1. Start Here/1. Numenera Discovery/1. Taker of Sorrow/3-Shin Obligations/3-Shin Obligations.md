# Shin Obligations Overview
 
Here and there in the [[The Steadfast - Player Map.jpg|Steadfast]], people sometimes take a pledge known as **shin obligation**. Pledges are predominantly taken in order to accomplish good works, though a few are made to fulfill the requirements of a legal penalty. A shin obligation ==commonly lasts a minimum of a few months== and usually never longer than a year. While under a pledge of shin obligation, a character might work locally to build a defensive wall, a home, or another needed structure in a community; foster a stranger or child in need for some period; take on another’s unfortunate debt with hard work; and so on. What’s important is that the character publicly pledges to shin obligation and the general task.

Some shin obligations involve travel. Pledged characters who travel commonly do so to reach another community that has special needs where they provide resources, know-how, or other aid. For instance, it’s not uncommon for someone with knowledge of metal working, numenera crafting, healing, or other specialized areas to travel to a small village or similar location to share what they know as their shin obligation pledge. And given how dangerous moving between isolated communities can be in the [[NinthWorld_AreaMap.jpg|Ninth World]], people sometimes pledge a shin obligation to provide protection to other travelers (as long as their purposes are at least somewhat noble).

The player characters become involved, and some may even meet each other, **because they all pledged to the same shin obligation** ==(*see note*)==: to protect the philosopher and aerosaltant known as [[Divine Nex]] on their tour of several outlying [[Navarene]] communities.

> [!NOTE] Note
> As part of a shin obligation pledge, the PC gives at least ==10 shins== to someone they trust and respect, or to a community elder. If the PC ever returns to claim the shins, it is understood that they have made good on their obligation. Shin obligations are in large part about trust, honor, and self-discovery.
