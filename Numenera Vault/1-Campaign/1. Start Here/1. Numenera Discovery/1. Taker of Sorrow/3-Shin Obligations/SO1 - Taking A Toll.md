*PCs are trapped in a spatial bubble and must deal with or defeat toll-taking [[Murden|murdens]] to get free*. 

The characters travel across a slanting, slippery plain that is weirdly smooth and sounds almost metallic under booted feet. They find a depression a few miles across that contains a pool, some vegetation, and some small game. 

##### **The Standoffish Hermit**: 
![[Creature - Abhuman (thumbnail).jpg|right]]
A hermit ([[Murden|murden]]) in a concealing black cloak also lives by the pool, but it doesn’t seem to want anything to do with the PCs—at least, not the first time the characters pass through. The hermit retreats to its intricately woven and rounded [[Bramble and Wire Thatched Hut|thatched hut]] when the PCs become aware that it is watching them, closing the metallic door that seals the entrance to make its desire to be left alone clear. Metallic wire is cunningly wound through the bramble and thatch, which makes the hut more protective than it might otherwise seem. The hermit tries to ignore the characters even if they make themselves a nuisance; it doesn’t want to reveal its true nature (see **An Offer of Aid** below) and strategy until the characters understand that their presence by the pool is a kind of trap.

```encounter
name: Hermit
creatures:
  - 1: [[Murden]]
```

##### **Spatial Redirection**: 
While making camp beside the pool might prove to be a nice rest, at some point the characters may become alarmed when they realize that somehow they’ve circled back and have returned to the pool. Each time they attempt to strike out in the right direction, they find themselves back at the pool. 

> [!faq] A successful ==difficulty 3 (9) Intellect task==, one that could be eased by training in understanding numenera, suggests that the depression in the landscape is some kind of spatial bubble separate from the rest of the normal world. Each time the characters attempt to leave, they are “bounced” back by the invisible walls of the reality bubble.

##### **An Offer of Aid**: 
Once the characters realize their situation, **the hermit leaves its residence and approaches them**. (*Alternatively, the PCs may take it upon themselves to see what the hermit knows before it can emerge.*) Upon closer examination, it’s clear that the hermit is **not human**, but an **[[abhuman]]** female of a **variety** known as a **[[murden]]**. ([[Creature - Murden.jpg|Show to Players]]) And she doesn’t live alone; **four** other murdens live in the thatched hut as an extended family unit.

```encounter
name: Abhumans
creatures:
  - 4: [[Murden]]
```

##### **The Matriarch’s Toll**: 
The matriarch (the “hermit”) ==doesn’t speak==, and her presence fills the air with mental static. She understands most human languages and can convey information through gestures or drawings in the drit in front of her hut. Using these methods, she conveys to the characters that she will open a way out for them, but only in return for gifts. The PCs can offer some ==gifts== (a working [[cypher]] or something of equal value suffices), ==intimidate== the murdens to let them go (though murdens don’t scare easy), or ==fight== them and hope that the interior of the hut will provide answers. The PCs might also decide to ==distract== the [[murdens]] while one of their number attempts to pilfer from them.

```encounter
name: Murdens
creatures:
  - 4: [[Murden]]
```

> [!faq] If the PCs give the murdens what they want, the matriarch indicates that the characters should wait. Then she enters the hut, uses a metallic device secreted there to temporarily deactivate the spatial bubble, and emerges, indicating (truthfully) that the PCs can go on their way. Whether or not the PCs believe her is on them.

##### **Murden Hut**: 
If investigated, the [[murden]] hut contains a horrific mess of ==random objects, bones, and colorful bits of cloth==. Finding anything of value requires a **minimum of ten minutes** of searching and probably a successful ==Intellect task that is difficulty 3 (9)==. In addition to a couple of [[Cypher|cyphers]], an [[oddity]], and **4d6 shins**, the characters find a smooth, fist-sized metallic device with a few depressible switches. If activated, the device collapses the reality bubble for several hours before it reforms. (*The device has no particular useful function outside the area where the spatial bubble is prone to form.*)
