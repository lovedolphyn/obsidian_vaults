*The PCs are setting up camp after a day of travel when they notice some movement.* 
**Read or paraphrase the following.**

> [!note]+ READ ALOUD 
> It’s good to finally rest and set up camp after spending most of the day moving. The camp rests in the shadow of a copse of angular trees that reflect light as if made of smooth bronze, more like sculptures than growing things. [[Divine Nex]]’s [[scarlet tent]] has already gone up, and the performer has retreated inside, despite there being no obvious entrance or exit. The weather is clear, and the last light of day sets fire to the clouds and the mountain tops to the northeast.
> 
> The peace is shattered by a man’s scream that echoes from a rubble field to the south. Instants later, two people break from the field at a full run, though one trips on a stone and falls, yelling this time in surprise. The other turns and darts back to help her compatriot. The fallen one is up again, now limping, but with his arm over his friend’s shoulder, they’re again racing away from the rubble field, toward the camp.

<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">The **two fleeing people**—a man and a woman—are dressed in simple but hardy clothing. Both are bloody, and one has several bleeding bite marks, which look serious though not immediately lethal. This is the one who fell. The other has a belt at which a few metallic tools are strapped, including a metallic scoop, a cutting implement, and a roll of tough synth tether. Both are obviously distraught</mark>, and if they spy the PCs, <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">they immediately begin begging for help</mark>.

At first, they are a bit incoherent in their fright and exhaustion. At the very least, the noise draws [[Divine Nex]] out, who immediately sees to their wounds, keeping up a constant verbal reassurance that <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“It’ll be all right, you’re going to be all right.”</mark>

When the two are calmed down somewhat, they explain that they are [[glister]] farmers from the nearby village of [[Vesburgh]]. ([[Divine Nex]] nods, murmuring,<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;"> “A place I was hoping to visit on this trip.”</mark>) Called [[Teel]] and [[Thosin]], they are newly wed, and work a plot of land south of [[Vesburgh]] left to [[Teel]] by her deceased parents. A plot of land that has come under direct attack from a newly established [[teratoma]] nest!

Seeing the PCs’ weapons, [[Teel]] directly asks, <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“Will you help us exterminate the nest? For [[Thosin]] and I, it is too much. But for you with your bows and swords, it will be as easy as digging up weeds!”</mark> 

>If asked to describe what a [[teratoma]] is, [[Thosin]] says <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“Separately, they are like horrible little lumps of nightmare. Together, they work like a pack of broken hounds to hunt. But instead of howling, they whisper or sing. No one knows why. It’s horrible.”</mark>

>If asked what they know about teratomas, the farmers explain that predators have always been something of a problem in [[Vesburgh]]. For the last five or so years, the village has been plagued by packs of horrific creatures that the locals call teratomas. For a long time, it was only a pack here and there, but in the last few years, the teratomas have multiplied so much that [[Vesburgh]] is in real danger. Each time an infestation is burned out, a new one appears a few weeks later.
