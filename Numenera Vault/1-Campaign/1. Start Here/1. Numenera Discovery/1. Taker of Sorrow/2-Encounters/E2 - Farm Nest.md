<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">**The farm** consists of a small two-room structure of stone and wood on a hill and a smaller separate structure for tools, including a plow that could be hooked up to an aneen. Vines on wooden X-shaped trellises surround the house and barn. There are no visible dead farm animals, because [[Teel]] managed to get them to run off when the [[Teratoma|teratomas]] attacked. </mark>

A pack of **seven** [[Teratoma, Mature|mature teratomas]] that have been feeding well for a few months, and thus have an ==average level of 4 instead of 3==, has established a **nest** beneath the barn. However, when the PCs first arrive on the scene, they might find the pack out in the vines eating the [[glister]] berries that were about ready for harvest, and spewing a cascade of nonsense: <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Are you my friend? I can’t feel my hands. The fat one was my favorite, but he’s gone. Where am I? Mother says don’t be late. Hello?</mark>
