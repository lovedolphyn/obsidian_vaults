>If the PCs travel to [[Gram]], they spy the “mountain” that hosts the community from at least 5 miles (8 km) away. The **entire slab is called Enigram Mountain**; the community built upon its face is [[Gram]].

>[!note] Read Aloud
>“There, do you see it?” asks [[Divine Nex]], pointing at a strange landscape feature [[img - View of Gram.jpg|Show to Players]]. The outcrop in the distance is too regular to be a natural formation. It looks almost like a great slab of reddish metal that was dropped from the sky and landed on its edge, embedding itself in the ground before toppling over, though it leans. A community sprawls across and up the steep face of the material, which is pocked and hollowed by hundreds of small and mid-sized cave mouths. The community fills a series of these hollows, many of which have been converted into dwellings, complete with wooden and stone walls, well-lighted connecting stairs zig-zagging up the face of the formation, and dozens of wide balconies. Stretching around the slab are several acres of cultivated fields and small homes of wood. 

![[img - View of Gram.jpg|right hsmall]]
The obvious way to “enter” the community is **through a gate** at the base that connects to a central stair, which ascends to the first level of connected hollows. Of course, the PCs might also decide to simply climb up the steep face, though due to its somewhat slippery nature, doing so is a ==difficulty 5 (15) task==.

##### **Approaching Gram**: 
As the characters approach, they witness an interesting sight. At least four [[teratoma]] packs (about twenty teratomas in all) simultaneously emerge from a narrow ravine and converge on the main gate, screaming, laughing, and uttering all manner of nonsense. **Three of the** **four** [[Gate Guard|gate guards]] stationed there draw bows defensively, but don’t fire. The **final guard** produces **two golden translucent rods that flash with silver lines and switches**. She raises them over her head until the tips touch. When they do, **a mighty blast of multi-threaded tones** that sound like **beautiful music** blares out. At least, it’s beautiful to the PCs. But it’s as if someone sprayed the teratomas with acid. They reverse course, screaming in utter anguish, and race away to the southwest. They may ==even pass the PCs, but unless the characters do something to interrupt== their headlong flight, they ignore the characters and keep running.

##### **Entering Gram**: 
Ninth Worlders who’ve heard rumors of the tranquility offered by the serein sometimes make their way to [[Gram]]. The guards assume the PCs, being strangers, number among those seeking solace. ==The guards provide directions== some number of flights up where the PCs can stay at the **supplicant hostel** to await the convenience of the serein. Alternatively, there are places to let in the **Market Round**, a large hollow open to the sky where **food**, **services**, and **goods** are bought and sold. ==([[Divine Nex]] prefers the latter, because the aerosaltant wants to ask around about setting up a performance.)==

>If the PCs **quiz the guards or random townspeople** about what’s been happening in [[Gram]], they get answers that are strangely emphatic: 
>- <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“Teratomas? Oh, we call them babble mites. They’re attracted to [[Gram]] because of all the places in the world, here there is no sorrow. I feel sorry for them.”</mark> 
>- <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“The devices we use to drive off babble mites were crafted in a city far to the east of here, in Ballarad, fashioned by a master tinkerer who called herself a Wright. We call them [[songbirds]].”</mark> 
>-<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;"> “No! Babble mites—or teratomas as you call them—don’t originate in [[Gram]]. Don’t be ridiculous. [[Gram]] is a place of tranquility. [[Weymel]] sees to that.”</mark> (When citizens of [[Gram]] speak [[Weymel]]’s name, most can’t help but smile at the thought of their leader.) 
>- <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“[[Weymel]] leads us. He is wise and witty. Beautiful. The best of us, and best loved. Everyone says so. He is head of the serein.” </mark>

>- <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“You sound troubled, as if you’re not at peace. You should go to the supplicant hostel and wait for the serein. Then you can try the [[Taker of Sorrow]], a balm for an anguished soul. Everyone here in [[Gram]] has gone to the Taker, and true to its name, it’s taken our burdens from us.”</mark>

Everyone in [[Gram]] is *friendly and warm*, **almost creepily so**. In fact, the PCs are invited into the **Town Round** (a wide, high, hollow area for **socialization** and **celebration**), there to be <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">feted with gifts of food and craft</mark> made in [[Gram]], and toasted many times **with crisp effervescent cider**.

Just by asking, the PCs can get someone to take them to meet [[Weymel]] (**after the welcome ceremony**). As it happens, [[Weymel]] can usually be found at the **supplicant hostel**, which lies **ten flights up**, and then **down a long corridor deep into the heart** of Enigram Mountain. 

##### **Divine Nex Goes on Ahead, Maybe**: 
[[Divine Nex]], upon hearing about the [[Taker of Sorrow]], is strongly affected. <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">The performer wants to **visit the supplicant hostel straight away** and slips away to do so if the PCs attend to other things first, such as staying longer in the Town Round, doing more investigation, or even just resting for an hour or more.</mark>
