<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">When the PCs go the **supplicant hostel** (whether that is immediately, or later), they find a wide, smooth chamber set with about a dozen low pallets, a wash basin, and a side area where one serein meditates. This is [[Nasi]], a young woman with a shaved head, a star tattooed on one cheek, and bright green robes. A **single shut door** leads further into the heart of Enigram Mountain.</mark> An *observant* character might notice that the door has been insulated to prevent the transmission of sound. 

> [!tip]+ GM Tip
> **Noticing** that the **door has been baffled** to **restrict** the transmission of **sound** is a ==difficulty 5 (15) task==.

[[Nasi]] ceases meditating to **greet** the newcomers. If the PCs ask about [[Weymel]] or the [[Taker of Sorrow]], <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">she glances at the sealed door. “[[Weymel]] is detained.” Which means access to the [[Taker of Sorrow]] is also impossible</mark>, she further indicates. But if the PCs would like to wait, they could meditate a bit on what burdens of sorrow and concern they’d most like to give up. 

While they wait, the PCs could ply [[Nasi]] with questions, which she answers [[E5 - Gram#^3a834b|in the same vein]] as the other residents of [[Gram]] presented earlier. She **doesn’t know** how long [[Weymel]] will be (==she glances again at the door==) but expects it’ll be **no more than a few hours**.

##### If Not With the PCs, Where Is [[Divine Nex]]?: 
<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">If the PCs didn’t immediately come to the supplicant hostel, but Nex did, the characters might be curious as to why the aerosaltant ==isn’t waiting in the hostel==. [[Nasi]] truthfully <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">claims she has never met the performer.</mark> ([[Nasi]] wasn’t here when Nex came through.)</mark>

##### Forcing the Door: 
If the PCs would prefer **not to wait**, they can attempt to **break down the** **[[Metallic Door, Barred|door]]**. It’s barred from the inside and made of the same red metal as the bulk of Enigram Mountain. If they do this in front of [[Nasi]], her eyes grow wide and she whispers, <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“The Reaper of Sin will come for you!”</mark> After that, ==she attempts to flee==, not wishing to see the Reaper in action.

##### Waiting for [[Weymel]]: 
After several hours, the **door is unbarred** from the inside and another serein appears, who talks with [[Nasi]] and **then is happy** to let the PCs back into the **chamber** to see [[Weymel]] and the [[Taker of Sorrow]]. The new serein, a youth called [[Mefin]], <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">indicates that yes, [[Weymel]] finished up with whatever was detaining him.</mark> (Which, unbeknownst even to [[Mefin]], was the latest failed application of the [[Taker of Sorrow]], resulting in another brood of teratomas that [[Weymel]] had to flush out of [[Gram]] with none the wiser.) Neither [[Nasi]] nor [[Mefin]] follow the PCs but instead **continue to meditate** in the supplicant hostel.
