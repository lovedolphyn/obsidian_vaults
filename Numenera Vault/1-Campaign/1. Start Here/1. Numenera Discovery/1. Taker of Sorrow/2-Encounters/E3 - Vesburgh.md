> [!tip]+ GM Tip
> People in [[Vesburgh]] know a little about [[Gram]] but don’t have much contact with the community. They (and [[Divine Nex]]) know that it houses a society of ascetics called the serein who practice a mental discipline designed to grant tranquility. 

<mark style="background: #FFF3A3A6;">Either with the farmers’ guidance or with directions provided by [[Divine Nex]], the PCs come to the **village of [[Vesburgh]]**. It’s composed of about a dozen multiroom wood and stone structures surrounding a central open square. However, crude palisades isolate about half the town (including the central square). It’s clear that the [[Vesburg Residents|residents]] have, for the most part, retreated into this protected area. The structures outside have been damaged, and two have been completely burned and destroyed. Several villagers move through the area outside the palisade, rebuilding the structures and cleaning up. As the PCs arrive, the **residents take up arms**, mostly bows and short swords.

A couple of [[Vesburgh Lookouts|humans stand on watchtowers]] (which are little more than tall, supported poles on which a small platform has been tied) peering over the palisades.</mark> ==These lookouts likely see the PCs coming unless the characters are trying to be stealthy.==

##### **A Chill Reception**: 
As it happens, **bandits** have recently tried to take advantage of [[Vesburgh]]’s problems, attacking the village a few times to see what they can acquire. But the PCs should be able to convince the villagers that they’re not bandits; [[Divine Nex]] provides an asset to such tasks. So does offering to help rebuild after the last bandit attack.

PCs who assist the villagers in a bit of cleanup and rebuilding turn a chill reception warm. After a few hours of work, the PCs are invited inside the palisade to share a meal and conversation. Sometime during this period, [[Divine Nex]] also offers to sing for the villagers, creating something of a spectacle in the small place. The performer also offers to provide a bit of healing for those who are suffering any ongoing issues. As it turns out, the aerosaltant is quite the hit.

>If the PCs don’t seem inclined to offer their aid (possibly because they don’t realize it is very much needed), [[Divine Nex]] asks them to throw in and help the villagers, saying that they can consider it part of their [[3-Shin Obligations|shin obligation]]. As the PCs talk to the villagers and move among them, they might meet the following people.

##### **Keersan, [[Vesburgh Lookouts|Vesburgh lookout]]**: 
A lanky twenty-some-year-old with fresh bandages and an irrepressible grin calls down to the PCs from a lookout tower, <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“What news from beyond the palisade?”</mark> and <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“Is that [[Divine Nex]]?”</mark> He is particularly interested in finding out when Nex will perform; it seems that news of the aerosaltant’s coming has preceded their arrival. If the PCs mention their run-in with the pack of tumorous lumps, <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Keersan grows solemn, nodding. He says that the scourge has only gotten worse, and he compares it to an epidemic. He says the worst is when you hear people talking outside your house, and you’re terrified to go out to check because you don’t know if it’s your neighbors or a couple of teratomas perched on your roof, waiting to pounce.</mark>

##### **Maxter, [[Teel]]’s grandfather**: 
If the PCs tell anyone of their interaction with [[Teel]] and [[Thosin]], an older man with a prodigious beard and a prosthetic leg (not obviously of the numenera) finds the PCs. <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">Maxter introduces himself as [[Teel]]’s grandfather and says he is worried for their safety.</mark> If he learns that the PCs helped them in any way, even if the PCs just accompanied the two to the safety of [[Vesburgh]], Maxter gives the characters <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">a thank-you gift: a bottle of [[glister]] wine (it’s quite good)</mark> <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">and an open offer of a place to stay in his home, which is not yet filled with other [[Vesburgh]] residents.</mark> <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">He says things haven’t been the same since [[Teel]]’s parents (one of which was his son) were killed by teratomas. After that, he doesn’t speak on that topic any further.</mark>

##### **Talosian, Aeon Priest**: 
[[Aeon Priest Talosian|Talosian]] came to [[Vesburgh]] only a few years ago, after the village had already repelled a few invasions of the strange new creatures that he dubbed “teratomas.” He finds the PCs because he is interested to hear if they encountered any of the creatures, how they dealt with them, and if the creatures said anything of interest. 

>If the PCs can recall anything, he pulls out a massive **journal** and records what they report. The book is full of similar notes of previous [[teratoma]] utterances.

>If the PCs are interested (and [[Divine Nex]] certainly is), Talosian reflects on his hypotheses. <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“The central question that’s bothered me is how these horrific beasts, so obviously unnatural and savage, can speak. Are they some breed of degenerate [[abhuman]]? But no, even abhumans cannot speak so well in the Truth. On the other hand, they don’t seem to speak in a way that makes any common sense. Yet they’re not mimicking what they hear from others. Each [[teratoma]] seems to have its own particular mannerisms. If one sings, it tends to always sing, and comes back to the same lyrics over and over. If another tells bad jokes, it’s always the same bad jokes. It’s almost as if each creature contains the seed of a splintered personality.”</mark>

What does it all mean? The Aeon Priest hasn’t put that together. However, Talosian does have a hypothesis that he fashioned from a recent cross-reference of all the information in his **teratoma journal**. ==The utterances, taken as a whole, seem to indicate that the teratomas have been exiled in some fashion, cast out, or driven away from their place of origin.== He thinks he’s narrowed it down to one of **three places**: **another dimension** entirely, **a ruin** (known as the [[E4 - Vault of Reflections|Vault of Reflections]]) about ==five days’ travel to the south==, and **a town** ([[Gram]]) about a half day’s travel to the northwest.

>The option of **looking into another dimension is far too wide open to consider seriously**, at least not without additional aid or some way to narrow things down, and Talosian can’t help with that. <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">The Aeon Priest admits he included the idea because of all the stories about creatures of various sorts being cast out of other worlds.</mark>

>If asked to provide his reasoning as to why both [[Gram]] and the [[E4 - Vault of Reflections|Vault of Reflections]] are implicated, Talosian says, <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">“Beyond the general theme of exile I discovered among the years of recorded utterances, two other notable themes are obvious: a loss of happiness and joy that, perhaps, these creatures once possessed. [[Gram]] has the serein, ascetics who promise to grant tranquility to supplicants. And the [[E4 - Vault of Reflections|Vault of Reflections]], according to what little lore I know about this prior-world structure, is said to erase all one’s worries merely with a look at the wonders that glitter inside.”</mark> 

>Finally, <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">he suggests that [[Gram]] is the more likely option because it is much closer.</mark> Creatures cast out from it might well make their way to [[Vesburgh]], while those cast out of the [[E4 - Vault of Reflections|Vault of Reflections]] might find some other community closer to it on which to predate. 

##### **Talosian’s Offer**: 
The Aeon Priest promises to provide the PCs with a <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">numenera trove</mark> that he keeps safe, as well as a <mark style="background: #BBFABBA6;">chest filled with shins</mark>, if they get to the bottom of the [[teratoma]] threat.
