<mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">The teacher, singer, philosopher, and inventor</mark> known as **Divine Nex** usually <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">wears a brilliant red and black cloak and one of several dozen masks. Some of them are beautiful, others are horrific, and many are sculpted to display a specific strong emotion like happiness, sadness, anger, or surprise. No one has ever seen Nex not in costume</mark>, because when they are not singing, reciting sublime poetry, teaching ethics or some other kind of philosophy, dancing with amazing grace, or imbibing tea or wine while engaged in an extended conversation over a camp fire, Nex <mark style="background: #ABF7F7A6;">retires to a large [[scarlet tent]] that has **no obvious exits or entrances**.</mark> Nex makes a living moving from community to community providing knowledge, entertainment, and news of distant locations. Sometimes Nex also serves as a messenger, if the message is important.

Joining Divine Nex on the road is to briefly become part of an ever-rotating cast of companions, friends, and guardians. Those who ally themselves with the performer—or as Nex likes to say, **“the aerosaltant”**—for even a brief time, make friends with Nex and often with each other. After that, fellow travelers typically go their own way to make room for a new entourage. In any event, they have stories likely to last a lifetime.

> [!info] 
> During the course of this adventure, the PCs are Nex’s guardians and entourage; there are no others. If a fight overwhelms the group, Nex aids the PCs by **providing a bit of healing** to each character ==(up to 2 points per round)== but doesn’t pick up arms or attack foes.

>**Divine Nex’s Travel Itinerary**: In addition to other small villages and towns, the aerosaltant wants to visit [[Vesburgh]]. So even if strange occurrences on the road didn’t lead the PCs there, *the performer would insist they go there first*.
